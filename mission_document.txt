==========================================================
1. THE MISSION, STATED PRECISELY
==========================================================
Build a closed-loop system in which:
  (a) a small initial capital base (Rs 2,00,000) is deployed against a
      systematically validated trading edge;
  (b) realised profit — never principal — funds progressively better
      infrastructure (cloud compute, data, eventually local hardware);
  (c) better infrastructure enables better research, which improves the
      edge, which increases profit;
  (d) the loop repeats, with each stage gated on evidence rather than
      on enthusiasm.

Success is NOT "a profitable month." Success is a system whose live
results are CONSISTENT WITH what its paper trading predicted, sustained
long enough that the consistency itself is statistically meaningful.
That consistency is the actual product. Profit is a consequence of it.

Explicitly in scope: changing asset class, changing market, operating
from outside India, and monetising the system itself rather than only
its returns (see Section 7).

2. SETTLED GROUND TRUTHS — DO NOT RE-DERIVE
==========================================================
Established by prior research and by direct measurement in this
project. Re-deriving these wastes effort; contradicting them requires
new evidence, not new argument.

2.1  Statistical mining fails at retail data volumes. A cross-
     correlation scan missed a signal deliberately embedded with a true
     8-day lag (found 0.03-0.07 correlation; horizon-matched detection
     picked lag 15 vs true 8). Hypotheses must come from a stated
     real-world mechanism, tested once — not discovered by scanning.

2.2  Costs dominate at high turnover. A correctly-specified strategy
     trading daily LOST money purely to friction (~5.5%/yr drag at 30
     round trips/yr against 0.38% round-trip cost). Only strategies
     under roughly 10-15 round trips/year survive with a modest edge.

2.3  Correlation-only strategies without a fundamental mechanism are
     fragile. Pairs/stat-arb showed +2.0%/yr, Sharpe 0.53 in training
     and -7.1%/yr, Sharpe -1.23 after a simulated regime break. Every
     tuned threshold failed identically.

2.4  Leverage multiplies volatility, not skill. On an identical 18%/yr
     strategy: 1x -> +91% median over 5 yrs; 3x -> +384%; 10x -> MEDIAN
     OUTCOME IS LOSING 80%, with 54.5% of simulated accounts wiped out.
     Leverage must be earned from measured live drawdown, never assumed.

2.5  HFT/latency arbitrage is closed to retail. Co-location and
     multi-crore infrastructure are table stakes. Do not pursue.

2.6  FIXED transaction costs dominate at small position sizes. Indian
     equity delivery round-trip cost is approximately
        0.222% of position  +  Rs 15.34 fixed (DP charge per scrip)
     which means: Rs 2,000 position -> 0.99% round trip;
     Rs 10,000 -> 0.375%; Rs 50,000 -> 0.253%.
     Any model using a flat percentage cost is wrong below ~Rs 25,000
     per position, and wrong in the dangerous direction.

2.7  At a small capital base, CONTRIBUTION RATE DOMINATES RETURN RATE.
     Rs 2 L at 18% for 10 years = Rs 10.5 L. The same with Rs 50,000/yr
     added = Rs 22.2 L. Improving 18% -> 25% is far harder, far riskier,
     and worth less than feeding the account.

2.8  Compute is not the bottleneck and is not scarce. The existing
     stack runs on GitHub Actions' free tier at Rs 0/month using ~90 of
     2,000 available minutes. There is 22x headroom. This workload is a
     few minutes of pandas per day.

2.9  CURRENT EVIDENCE STATE: 35 days of live paper history, 20
     contestants, equity range 0.876 to 1.011. Zero promotions. NO EDGE
     HAS BEEN DEMONSTRATED. At this sample size the spread is
     indistinguishable from noise. Everything built so far is
     machinery awaiting evidence.

4. TRANSACTION COST AND TAX MODEL (INDIA)
==========================================================
VERIFY ALL OF THIS WITH A CA. Rates change; this reflects a knowledge
cutoff and is intended as a modelling starting point, not advice.

4.1 TRANSACTION COSTS — Indian equity delivery, discount broker
    Brokerage (delivery)     Rs 0 at several brokers
    STT                      0.10% buy + 0.10% sell
    Exchange txn charge      ~0.00297% per side (NSE)
    SEBI turnover fee        Rs 10 per crore
    Stamp duty               0.015% on buy
    GST                      18% on (brokerage + txn + SEBI charges)
    DP charge                ~Rs 15.34 per scrip per sell day  <-- FIXED
    => round trip ~= 0.222% of position + Rs 15.34

    MODELLING REQUIREMENT: any backtest or promotion rule MUST use a
    size-aware cost function. A flat percentage is materially wrong
    below Rs 25,000 per position. This is currently a live defect in
    factory.py (COST_PER_SIDE is flat 0.0019).

4.2 DIRECT TAX — India
    Equity delivery, held <= 12 months (STCG)   20%
    Equity delivery, held  > 12 months (LTCG)   12.5%, with a
                                                Rs 1.25 lakh/yr
                                                exemption
    Intraday equity        Speculative business income -> slab rates
    F&O                    Non-speculative business income -> slab
                           rates; business expenses deductible
    Crypto / VDA           30% flat + cess; 1% TDS on EVERY transfer;
                           NO loss set-off; no expense deduction
                           beyond cost of acquisition
    Foreign shares         Slab rate if held <= 24 months;
                           12.5% LTCG beyond 24 months
    Dividends              Taxed at slab

    AFTER-TAX MODELLING REQUIREMENT: the tournament currently compares
    strategies on PRE-TAX returns. A strategy with a 6-month average
    hold taxed at 20% and a strategy with a 13-month hold taxed at
    12.5% are not comparable on gross Sharpe. Post-tax expectancy must
    become a first-class metric before any promotion decision funds
    real capital.

4.3 THE HOLDING-PERIOD CLIFF — a genuine, large, legal edge
    Crossing the 12-month line changes the tax rate from 20% to 12.5%
    AND unlocks a Rs 1.25 lakh annual exemption. On a Rs 2 lakh account
    this exemption alone can cover the ENTIRE annual gain, taking the
    effective tax rate to zero.

    Worked: Rs 2 L growing 20% = Rs 40,000 gain.
      Held < 12 months:  20% STCG            -> Rs 8,000 tax
      Held > 12 months:  within Rs 1.25 L    -> Rs 0 tax
    That is a 20% improvement in net return achieved purely by holding
    period, with no improvement in the strategy whatsoever.

    STRATEGIC IMPLICATION, and it is a big one: this project's own
    research (2.2) already says only low-turnover strategies survive
    costs. Tax law pushes in the SAME direction, hard. A strategy
    holding positions >12 months is favoured twice over — once by
    friction, once by tax. The design space should be biased strongly
    toward long-hold, low-turnover, mechanism-driven positions.
    Everything the operator has learned independently points the same
    way. Follow it.

5. LEGITIMATE STRUCTURAL ADVANTAGES — AND THE BRIGHT LINES
==========================================================
The operator asked about "loopholes." Here is the honest version.

The genuinely large advantages available are ORDINARY, LEGAL, and
BORING. They are also worth more than anything clever:

  5.1  Holding-period management (Section 4.3). Largest single lever
       available. Legal, uncontroversial, enormous at small size.
  5.2  Harvesting the Rs 1.25 lakh LTCG exemption every financial year.
       Unused exemption does not carry forward — use it or lose it.
  5.3  Tax-loss harvesting before 31 March. Realise losses to offset
       realised gains; STCL can offset both STCG and LTCG.
  5.4  Carry-forward of losses (8 years) if the ITR is filed on time.
       Filing late forfeits this — a pure paperwork own-goal.
  5.5  If trading is treated as business income (F&O, intraday),
       genuine business expenses are deductible: internet, electricity,
       hardware depreciation, data subscriptions, advisory fees. Keep
       clean records from day one.
  5.6  Choosing delivery over intraday keeps income out of the
       speculative-business classification and inside the capital-gains
       regime, which at these sizes is usually far more favourable.
  5.7  Entity structuring (LLP / private limited) becomes worth
       modelling only at much larger scale, where the flat corporate
       rate may beat personal slab rates. Not relevant at Rs 2 lakh;
       revisit around G4.
  5.8  GIFT City IFSC offers real tax concessions but is oriented to
       institutional and NRI participants. Worth understanding, almost
       certainly not accessible or worthwhile at this scale.

THE BRIGHT LINES — do not cross these, and do not ask an AI to help
cross them. Each one carries consequences that dwarf any conceivable
gain on a Rs 2 lakh account:

  X  Non-disclosure of foreign assets (ITR Schedule FA). Black Money
     Act penalties are severe and are assessed per year of
     non-disclosure. If you ever hold US equities, disclose. Always.
  X  Trading offshore forex or binary-options platforms as a resident
     — FEMA violation, and these platforms are frequently outright
     fraudulent.
  X  Structuring remittances to circumvent LRS limits.
  X  Under-reporting or non-reporting of crypto gains. The 1% TDS
     regime means the tax department already has the transaction
     record. There is nothing to hide behind.
  X  Trading on material non-public information.
  X  Any form of market manipulation — spoofing, layering, wash
     trading, circular trading.
  X  Using another person's PAN, demat, or bank account.
  X  Misrepresenting a paper track record as a live one to anyone,
     ever — especially if Section 7 is ever pursued.

The realistic assessment: there is no clever structure that turns a bad
edge into a good one, and no tax trick worth the tail risk of a Black
Money Act assessment. The legal levers in 5.1-5.4 are large, immediate,
and sufficient. Take those. Ignore the rest.

  predict has NOT passed. It got lucky, and luck does not scale.

  Action: increase capital via ladder promotion AND via contribution
  (per 2.7 — contribution is the dominant lever at this size).

  Gate to advance: G3 (Rs 25 lakh) with a track record intact.

PHASE 4 — RECONSIDER VENUE AND STRUCTURE

7. THE HONEST ANSWER TO "SMALL SUM -> LARGE SUM"
==========================================================
The operator said: "we are not chasing something small." That deserves
a direct answer rather than a reassuring one.

Pure compounding from Rs 2 lakh:
    at 18% net -> Rs 1 crore in ~23.6 years
    at 25% net -> Rs 1 crore in ~17.5 years
    at 35% net -> Rs 1 crore in ~13.0 years  (sustained 35% at retail
                                              scale for 13 years is
                                              essentially unheard of
                                              without leverage, and
                                              2.4 forecloses leverage)

So: trading returns alone, on this capital base, do not produce a large
sum on any timescale the operator will find acceptable. That is the
arithmetic. Any plan that ignores it is a fantasy.

There are exactly three honest routes to a large sum, and they are
complementary rather than exclusive:

  ROUTE A — CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION (per 2.7)
    The dominant lever at small size. Rs 2 L + Rs 2 L/yr at 18% reaches
    Rs 57 L in 10 years and Rs 3.5 Cr in 20 — versus Rs 10.5 L and
    Rs 55 L with no contributions. The trading system's job at this
    stage is to EARN THE RIGHT to receive larger contributions by
    proving an edge exists.

  ROUTE B — TIME AND SURVIVAL
    Unglamorous and genuinely powerful. The overwhelming majority of
    retail algo projects die from a blown account or from abandonment,
    not from insufficient cleverness. A modest, honest, surviving edge
    compounding for 20 years beats a spectacular one that ends in
    year 3. The Three Laws already encode this instinct — they are the
    project's best feature. Protect them.

  ROUTE C — MONETISE THE SYSTEM, NOT ONLY ITS RETURNS
    This is the route that actually answers "not chasing something
    small." A genuinely validated systematic track record is worth far
    more than the returns it generates on Rs 2 lakh, because it can be
    applied to capital that is not yours. In India the legitimate,
    regulated paths include: SEBI-registered Investment Adviser (RIA),
    SEBI-registered Research Analyst, Portfolio Management Services
    (PMS — high net-worth and capital requirements), and AIF Category
    III. Each has real registration thresholds, compliance obligations,
    and capital or qualification requirements that must be researched
    properly before being counted on.
    The prerequisite for ALL of them is identical: an auditable, honest,
    multi-year track record. Which is precisely what this project is
    already designed to produce.
    Note the implication — the evidence machine is the valuable asset.
    Not the Rs 2 lakh. Build the evidence machine well.

The synthesis: use trading to PROVE an edge at small scale. Use
contribution and time to grow the base. If and only if the edge proves
real and durable, use the resulting track record — through properly
registered channels — to access capital far beyond Rs 2 lakh. That is
the realistic path from small to large. There is no shortcut, and every
apparent shortcut is either leverage (2.4), overfitting (2.1/2.3), or
fraud.

8. KILL CONDITIONS — WHEN TO STOP
==========================================================
Written now, before the data arrives, precisely so they cannot be
rationalised away later. This is Law 1 applied to the project itself.

Stop, or fundamentally restructure, if:
  * After 12 months of live paper trading, no strategy clears the
    existing RULES bar.
  * After 12 months, the best contestant fails to beat a Nifty ETF net
    of the corrected size-aware cost model and applicable tax. (In this
    case the correct action is explicit: buy the index, and redirect
    the effort. This is a SUCCESSFUL outcome for an evidence machine.)
  * Promoted strategies systematically underperform their paper results
    once real costs apply — i.e. paper does not predict live. This
    invalidates the entire promotion methodology, not just one
    strategy.
  * The advisor trust weight decays to its floor (0.05), indicating
    advisor-evolved children consistently fail to beat the parents they
    replaced.
  * Real drawdown exceeds the modelled maximum by a wide margin,
    indicating the risk model is wrong in the dangerous direction.
  * The operator finds himself wanting to change RULES, LADDER, or
    COST_PER_SIDE to make results look better. That impulse is the
    single most reliable signal that the edge is not real.

An evidence machine that cannot return "no" is not an evidence machine.
It is an expensive way to confirm what you already wanted to believe.

10. STANDING CONSTRAINTS
==========================================================
Inherited from the project constitution. Binding on any AI or human
working on this system.

  * The Three Laws: hypotheses before testing; live strategies never
    mutated, only replaced by children starting at rung 0; capital
    earned through the ladder, never granted.
  * Never change RULES, LADDER, or COST_PER_SIDE without the
    operator's explicit, separate, in-session instruction. These are
    financial risk parameters, not code-quality knobs.
    (Note: correcting COST_PER_SIDE to be size-aware is a CORRECTNESS
    fix, not a risk-appetite change — but it still requires explicit
    sign-off, because it changes every historical verdict.)
  * Never add options, futures, margin, or leverage logic.
  * Never add or modify broker/execution/API-key code without explicit
    instruction.
  * Never enable real-money execution autonomously. Funding a rung is
    always a deliberate human action.
  * Never merge to main, push to main, or force-push without fresh,
    explicit, in-session authorisation. A previous session's approval
    does not carry forward.
  * Never present synthetic or simulated results as live results.
  * Never claim a test was run against real market data when it was
    not.

11. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH VERSION
==========================================================
We have built a disciplined evidence machine and it has produced 35
days of data, which is nothing. The machinery is well ahead of the
evidence. Before any capital moves, the cost model must be made
size-aware and the tax treatment must be modelled, because both are
currently wrong in the direction that flatters results. Then the
correct action is to wait — twelve months of honest paper trading,
adding nothing — and see whether anything beats a Nifty ETF after real
costs and real tax. Infrastructure needs no money until roughly
Rs 25 lakh of capital, because the current stack is free and 95%
unused. Meanwhile the dominant growth lever is not a better algorithm;
it is contribution and time. And if a genuine edge does emerge, the
valuable asset will not be the returns on Rs 2 lakh — it will be the
auditable track record, which through properly registered channels can
be applied to capital far larger than our own. That is the realistic
path from a small sum to a large one.
