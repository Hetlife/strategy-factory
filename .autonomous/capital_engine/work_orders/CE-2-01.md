## Required contents (template; fill every field)
1. **Mechanism (one paragraph, no numbers from data):** diversified carry across uncorrelated low-volatility sleeves (cash-like yield, sovereign duration, gold as INR/crisis hedge, broad equity beta) with low turnover; return is mostly yield + rebalancing bonus, not forecasting.
2. **Instruments (NSE-listed ETFs only, so the existing cost model applies):** one liquid/overnight ETF, one gold ETF, one G-sec / target-maturity ETF, one Nifty 50 ETF. Use the tickers and expense ratios from `docs/capital_engine/research/R2_india_regulatory_venues.md`; if a ticker is not there, say UNKNOWN — do not guess.
3. **Rules stated in advance:** fixed target weights (proposal: 40% liquid, 20% G-sec, 20% gold, 20% Nifty 50), band rebalancing at ±5 pp absolute, checked weekly in `report()`, never more than one rebalance per week. Rationale: Vanguard's rebalancing evidence (R1 §6) says more frequent gives no risk-adjusted gain and costs more.
4. **Pre-committed expectations and kill conditions:** expected ~repo rate + 1-2%/yr; max drawdown expectation < 8%; if realised max DD > 12% or the sleeve trails the liquid ETF over 24 months, the hypothesis is FALSIFIED — write what happens then (default: 100% liquid ETF).
5. **What would make us wrong:** a gold/equity/duration co-crash (2022-style, see R1 §6).
6. **Benchmark contestant:** the liquid ETF itself (the "do nothing" bar).
7. Sign-off line for Het: "approved for paper trading as class A profile `shield_nse`". Until he signs, no profile is enabled.
