## Required contents
1. Mechanism: slow-moving capital flows, herding and the absence of circuit breakers produce multi-week trends; drawdown control comes from being flat in downtrends, not from forecasting.
2. Universe: BTC, ETH + up to 8 more by 1-year average volume, fixed list reviewed quarterly (no mid-quarter additions — survivorship guard). Universe choice logged with date.
3. Rules (parameters bounded by mechanism, NOT fitted): Donchian/breakout or moving-average-cross with lookbacks between 50 and 200 days only; long-or-flat (no shorting, no leverage — hard rule); position = equal weight across coins in uptrend, cash otherwise; volatility cap per coin at 2× BTC's trailing 90-day vol. Rebalance at most weekly.
4. Costs modelled: 0.2-0.5% round trip exchange fee + 0.1% spread (R2 figures), 1% TDS on every sale as a cash drag, 30% tax on gains with NO loss set-off computed per financial year in the class's `post_tax_expectancy`.
5. Benchmarks: BTC buy-and-hold (the "just hold" bar) AND the cash baseline (B0). A contestant promotes only if it beats BOTH on the promotion gate.
6. Pre-committed falsification: if after 12 months of paper the best contestant's post-tax excess over BTC buy-and-hold is ≤ 0 or its max DD > 40%, class B is FALSIFIED → stays paper indefinitely, no real capital.
