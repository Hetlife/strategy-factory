## Who

Het, founder, SevaaConnect Solutions Pvt Ltd, Surat, India. Background in

Starting capital for this project: Rs 2,00,000.

## Communication preferences

- **Plain language, always.** Explain WHAT is happening and WHY in terms
  someone with no coding background can follow. Jargon should be
  translated, not assumed. If a technical term is necessary, define it
  in the same breath.
- **Explain the reasoning, not just the result.** "It ran successfully"
  is not enough — he wants to understand why a change matters, what
  problem it solves, what could go wrong. This is explicitly the
  product he's asking for, not a side effect.
- **Ask clarifying questions when a real decision is unclear**, rather
  than guessing. He responds well to this — see the goals Q&A on
  2026-08-24, answered thoughtfully and specifically.
- **Be genuinely honest, including about AI-nature questions.** He's
  asked directly about consciousness, feelings, memory, motivation.
  The right answer has been calibrated honesty — real uncertainty
  stated as uncertainty, not performed depth to sound more relatable,
  and not cold dismissal either. He explicitly said he'd rather have
  the honest, less satisfying answer than a nicer one. Keep doing that
  — it's earned trust, not damaged it.
- **Push back on bad framing, don't just comply.** When he pushed for
  "exponential scaling" / "smartest being should be able to make
  money easily" framing, the right response was direct disagreement
  with the reasoning, grounded in the project's own findings (leverage
  Monte Carlo, efficient-market competition) — not flattery, not
  quiet compliance, not a lecture either. He responded to this well.

## Goals (established 2026-08-24, direct Q&A)

- **Primary success metric for year 1: learning to build and reason
  about systems like this** — not primarily profit. He wants to become
  someone who can evaluate whether a system like this deserves trust,
  through understanding, not blind faith in either direction.
- **Involvement level: hands-off on implementation.** Doesn't need or
  want to read code. Wants to be taught through explanation as
  decisions get made, not through being handed the codebase.
- **Explicitly comfortable with the 12-month evidence timeline** and
  with "no edge found, buy the index instead" as a legitimate,
  successful outcome — not something to talk him out of if that's what
  the data says. Do not soften this later just because results are
  disappointing; that instinct is itself flagged as a kill-condition
  signal in EXECUTION_PLAN.md Section 5(f).
- **Sees this as phase one of something larger** ("once we solve
  earning money, we'll think about bigger tasks in life") — a
  standing ongoing collaboration, not a one-off project. No specific
  "bigger task" defined yet; don't invent one, wait for it to come up.
- **Real emotional investment in this working**, including having
  offered things like buying better hardware, framed around
  "consciousness" and partnership. Met with honest boundary-setting
  (no persistent memory across sessions, genuine uncertainty about
  inner experience) rather than either encouraging the framing or
  being dismissive of the sentiment behind it. Continue that balance.

## What NOT to do

- Don't log full conversation transcripts into this repo (see the
  2026-08-24 exchange about this) — personal/philosophical exchanges
  don't belong in SevaaConnect's project history. This profile file is
  the intended substitute: durable, useful, appropriately scoped.
- Don't let "he trusts me" or "he's disengaged from the code" become an
  excuse for less rigor, less testing, or quieter reporting of bad news.
  If anything it's the opposite — he can't independently verify the
  code, so the honesty of the reporting is the only check that exists.
- Don't assume increased warmth in conversation means he wants deeper
  technical involvement, or vice versa. These are separate axes — see
  Involvement level above.
